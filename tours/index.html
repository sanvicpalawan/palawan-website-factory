02 · The Day

What a day looks like.

Tour A, door to door. Times shift with the tide and the weather — this is the shape of it.

  1. 08:30
    Pick-up in El NidoWe collect from any hotel or hostel in town. Life vests and dry bags are already on board.
  2. 09:15
    First swimA sheltered reef to get comfortable in the water before the lagoon.
  3. 10:30
    Big LagoonKayak through the crack in the cliff. No motors inside — just you and the limestone.
  4. 12:30

  5. 14:00

  6. 16:00
    Back to El NidoCold towels, fresh fruit and a drop at your door.

05 · Before You Ask

Straight answers.

What should I bring?+

Swimwear, a hat, reef-safe sunscreen and a dry bag. We provide life vests, snorkel gear, towels and lunch.

Is it safe for non-swimmers?+

Yes. Everyone wears a life vest, guides swim with the group, and we choose sheltered sites when the swell is up.

Can I book privately?+

Any tour can be chartered for your group. Send us the date and the number of guests for a quote.

What if it rains?+

Palawan rain passes quickly. We run tours in light rain and reschedule or refund if the coast guard closes the bay.

How do I book?+

WhatsApp us with the tour code, the date and the number of guests. We hold seats for 24 hours.
