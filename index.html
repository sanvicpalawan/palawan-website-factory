03 · The Story

Built by a family that kept the lease.

Daloy began as a single espresso machine in a former sunduke shop on Rizal Avenue. The family who ran it refused three offers for the lease and instead spent a year learning to roast.

The room is still owned by them. The coffee comes from three small farms in the Cordillera, the produce from Palawan farms we can name, and the lechon from a supplier whose pigs we have met. Nothing arrives that we cannot explain.

  • RoastedWeekly, on the island, in a 12kg drum
  • ProduceFrom 14 named farms in Palawan
  • Staff18 people, most hired from the neighbourhood

Open monday — friday 07:00–23:00, and later on weekends. Walk-ins welcome at the bar; tables are held for reservations.

07 · NFC & QR

The little card on every table.

Each table carries a small NFC card and a printed QR. Tap or scan and the island’s menu, ordering and loyalty live in your browser — no app, no account, no friction.

  • View the menu No app, no sign-up. The menu opens in the browser.
  • Order & pay Send your order to the kitchen and settle at the table.
  • Join the loyalty Six stamps on a card that lives on your phone.
  • Leave a review Thirty seconds, straight to the manager’s inbox.

Try it now. Scan the code, or tap your phone against the card on table 14.

Destination links are managed from the client dashboard. Repoint a card at any time without reprinting it.

Every card is unique. Analytics are privacy-conscious — counts, not identities.
