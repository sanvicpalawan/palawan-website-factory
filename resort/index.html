01 · The Island

Luxury, measured by what it leaves behind.

Amanpulo was built on a simple agreement: the island would be a guest, not a host. Every structure sits inside the tree line, every path was laid by hand.

Solar arrays and battery storage carry the island after dark. Rainwater is captured, filtered and returned to the ground. The reef — seven kilometres of it — is patrolled, planted and left alone to do what reefs do best.

03 · Explore

Three worlds, one shoreline.

Reef, lagoon and limestone forest — each with its own guide, its own boat and its own hour of the day.

The House Reef

Seven kilometres of coral garden begins ten metres from the north beach. Turtles, clownfish and reef sharks are regulars.

Snorkel · 2 hrs

The Hidden Lagoons

Kayak into lagoons with no entrance by boat. The water inside is colder, greener, and entirely yours for an hour.

Kayak · 3 hrs

The Forest Trail

A ridge path through limestone forest with the resident naturalist. Hornbills overhead, monitor lizards underfoot.

Hike · 2.5 hrs

The West Beach

Four kilometres of sand with no buildings on it. Walk it at dusk, when the light goes amber and the day boats have gone.

Walk · 90 min

04 · Wellness

The body remembers the tide.

Wellness here is not a programme. It is the default setting — salt air, early light, and a schedule that bends around the sun rather than the clock.

  • Sunrise movementYoga, breathwork and guided swims on the north beach, daily at 06:15.
  • The Reef SpaFour open-air pavilions above the water. Products made on the island from coconut, calamansi and sea salt.
  • Forest bathingA guided walk through the limestone forest with the island’s resident naturalist.
